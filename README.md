# 🚀 RepairRouteAI

### Smarter Repairs. Better Decisions.

RepairRouteAI is an AI-assisted repair platform that helps users understand device problems, estimate repair costs, compare repair vs replacement, discover technicians, schedule repairs, and maintain digital repair history.

---

## 💡 Problem

When a device breaks, users often don't know:

- What caused the problem
- How much the repair may cost
- Whether repairing or replacing is better
- Which technician to trust
- How to track the repair
- Whether the device is still under warranty

This can lead to unnecessary expenses, delays, and poor repair decisions.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

RepairRouteAI brings the entire repair journey into one platform.

Users can:

1. Describe their device problem
2. Get an AI-assisted preliminary assessment
3. View possible causes
4. Get an estimated repair-cost range
5. Compare repair vs replacement
6. Discover technicians
7. Compare repair quotes
8. Schedule a repair
9. Track repair progress
10. Maintain digital repair and warranty history

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Diagnosis
Analyze device symptoms and receive possible causes and recommended next steps.

### 💰 Repair Cost Estimation
Get an indicative repair-cost range based on the device and reported problem.

### ⚖️ Repair vs Replace
Compare repair and replacement factors before making a decision.

### 👨‍🔧 Technician Discovery
Find technicians based on specialization, rating, availability, and service area.

### 📋 Quote Comparison
Compare technician quotes including parts, labour, warranty, and estimated completion time.

### 📅 Repair Scheduling
Schedule a repair appointment with a selected technician.

### 🔄 Repair Tracking
Track the repair journey from request submission to completion.

### 🧾 Digital Repair History
Maintain a record of previous repairs, costs, parts, and technicians.

### 🛡️ Warranty Management
Store warranty information and receive expiry reminders.

## 🧠 AI Architecture

RepairRouteAI uses an AI service layer for:

- Preliminary problem assessment
- Possible cause identification
- Repair-cost estimation
- Repair recommendations
- Repair vs replacement analysis

AI results are intended as preliminary guidance and should not replace professional inspection.
